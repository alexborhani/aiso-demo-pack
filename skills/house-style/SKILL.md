# House style

- Plain words, short sentences, one idea per paragraph.
- Say who it affects, what changes, and when, in the first three lines.
- Give a contact for questions, never a generic mailbox.
- No exclamation marks. No "please be advised".
- Dates as 14 October 2026; amounts with a currency, never a bare number.
