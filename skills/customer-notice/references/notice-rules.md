# Customer notice rules (Meridian Works, Customer Operations)

- **Price changes:** at least 30 days' notice before the effective date (standard terms, clause 7.2).
  Name the clause in the notice.
- **Service interval changes:** at least 14 days' notice; say which models are affected.
- **Parts and kits:** say the old and the new part number, and until when the old one ships.
- **Contact:** always the service desk, service@meridianworks.example, +44 1632 960 411, 07:00 to 19:00 on working days.
  Never a named person, never a no-reply address.
- **Sign-off:** Customer Operations signs off every notice before it is filed. The administrator on duty
  signs off in AI Stackops.
