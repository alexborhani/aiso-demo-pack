---
type: policy
title: Expenses
tags: ["finance", "travel"]
---
Claim expenses within 30 days through the Finance portal with a receipt. Travel is economy under six hours and premium economy above. Meals are covered at up to 45 per day when travelling. Anything over 500 needs a manager's approval before it is spent.

Personal cards are reimbursed within ten working days of approval. Company cards are issued to people who travel more than twice a month.
