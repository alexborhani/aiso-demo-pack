type: procedure
title: IT basics for everyone
tags: ["it"]
---
Every colleague gets a laptop, a Meridian account and access to the tools their role needs on day one. Passwords are managed by the password manager; multi-factor sign-in is required everywhere. Lost or stolen equipment is reported to the helpdesk immediately, day or night.

The helpdesk answers within one working hour during core hours. Software requests go through the catalogue; anything not in it needs a security review first.
