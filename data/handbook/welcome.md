Meridian Works designs and services industrial pumps and the control systems around them, from three sites: the Halden plant, the Riverside service centre and the Crestview office. We have about 400 people.

This handbook is public. It tells new colleagues how the company works, what we expect of each other and where to find help. Nothing here is confidential; if you are looking for pay, benefits detail or personal records, those live with People Operations.
