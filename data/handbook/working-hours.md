type: policy
title: Working hours and leave
tags: ["leave", "hours"]
---
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time; teams agree the rest. Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays, rising to 28 after five years. Leave is booked in the People portal at least two weeks ahead for anything over three days.

Sick leave is self-certified for up to five consecutive days. Beyond that, People Operations asks for a note. Nobody is expected to read email while on leave.
