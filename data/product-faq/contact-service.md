The Meridian Works service desk is open 07:00 to 19:00 on working days: telephone +44 1632 960 411, email service@meridianworks.example, or the MeridianLink app under Support. For a pump that has stopped in production, the 24-hour emergency line is +44 1632 960 499; a field engineer is dispatched from the Riverside service centre or the nearest partner within four hours under a service contract, and by the next working day otherwise. Have the pump serial number, the model, and the MW-Control fault code ready. Training courses for operators run monthly at Riverside.
