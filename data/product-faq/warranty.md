Every new pump carries a 24-month warranty from the date of commissioning, or 30 months from dispatch, whichever ends first. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including the mechanical seal in the first 12 months. It does not cover wear parts after 12 months (seals, liners, impellers on slurry duty), damage from running dry, cavitation from a starved suction, pumping fluids outside the datasheet, or repairs by anyone other than Meridian Works or an authorised service partner. Warranty claims go through the service desk with the pump serial number and the MW-Control log export; most claims are decided within five working days. A service contract extends the warranty to 36 months.
