Meridian Works makes three industrial pump ranges. The MW-300 is a single-stage centrifugal pump for clean water and light process duty, 5 to 45 kW, flows to 180 cubic metres an hour. The MW-500 is a multi-stage pump for high-head duty such as boiler feed and booster stations, 15 to 160 kW, heads to 400 metres. The MW-700 is a heavy-duty slurry pump with a hardened iron casing for mining and aggregates. Every model ships with the MW-Control panel, which reports vibration, bearing temperature and seal condition over Modbus or the MeridianLink app.
