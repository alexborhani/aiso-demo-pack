title: Recommended spares
tags: ["spares"]
---
Keep on site for each MW-300: one mechanical seal kit, one set of bearings, one casing gasket set, one coupling insert. For each MW-500: seal kit, bearing set, wear-ring set, one stage gasket set, coupling insert. For each MW-700: two liner sets, one impeller, one seal kit, gland packing, bearing set. Spares are ordered from the service desk with the pump serial number; standard parts ship within two working days from Riverside, and a critical-spares agreement guarantees next-day delivery. Genuine parts carry a 12-month warranty; non-genuine parts void the pump warranty.
