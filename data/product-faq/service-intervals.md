title: Service intervals
tags: ["service"]
---
MW-300: inspect and grease bearings every 2,000 running hours or six months; replace the mechanical seal every 8,000 hours or two years; full overhaul at 24,000 hours. MW-500: bearings every 1,500 hours or six months; seal every 6,000 hours; overhaul at 20,000 hours, with a wear-ring check at 10,000 hours. MW-700: liner and impeller wear check every 500 hours; seal water flow checked every shift; overhaul at 6,000 hours. The MW-Control panel raises a service reminder at 90 per cent of each interval. A service contract from the Riverside service centre covers every interval and the parts.
