CONFIDENTIAL — Finance and the leadership team. Not for release before the board meeting of 28 October.

Preliminary Q3 revenue is 41.8 million, six per cent below plan, on lower service volumes at Riverside. Gross margin held at 18.2 per cent. Cash at quarter end is 9.4 million. The Nordvik Water contract, signed in September, adds an estimated 3.1 million a year from Q1. The full close package follows the usual timetable; these figures may still move by up to half a per cent.
