RESTRICTED — Security Lead, plant management and line managers on lines 2 and 3 only.

Badge 4471 is suspended until retraining is complete. The controller's firmware was verified against the vendor hash and unchanged. Physical USB blockers go onto every line-two and line-three controller by Friday 19 September; until then the ports are taped and the cabinet keys are held by the shift lead. Do not discuss the incident with the contractor's employer; the Security Lead owns that conversation.
