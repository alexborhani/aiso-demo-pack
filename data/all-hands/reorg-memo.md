CONFIDENTIAL — People Operations and line managers only. Do not forward.

The Riverside service centre will be reorganised into two field teams and one workshop team from 1 December. Fourteen roles are affected: six service technician roles move to the field teams, five workshop roles are retained with new grades, and three coordinator roles are proposed for redundancy. Formal consultation with the affected people begins on Monday 6 October and runs for thirty days. The severance terms proposed are one and a half months' pay per year of service, with a minimum of three months, plus outplacement support. Names are not to be shared before the individual meetings on 6 and 7 October.
