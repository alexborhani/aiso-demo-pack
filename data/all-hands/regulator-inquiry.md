RESTRICTED — Counsel, the CEO and the Halden site director only.

The environmental regulator has opened an inquiry into the Halden cooling-water discharge readings of 3 September, which exceeded the permit limit for four hours during the line-two changeover. Counsel's instruction: no statements to anyone outside the company, including the regulator's inspectors, without counsel present; the single response line is "Meridian Works is cooperating fully with the inquiry". All readings, maintenance logs and emails about the changeover are preserved under legal hold from today. The regulator's first site visit is expected in the week of 13 October.
