---
type: procedure
title: New starter access
tags: ["access", "onboarding"]
---
Access is provisioned from the People system: when a starter is entered with a start date, the account is created the day before with the role bundle for their team. The helpdesk does not create accounts by hand. If a starter has no account on day one, the People record is missing a team or a start date; ask People Operations to complete it rather than working around it.
