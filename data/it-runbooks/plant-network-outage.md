---
type: procedure
title: Plant network outage
tags: ["plant", "network"]
---
The Halden plant floor runs on its own network segment. If the floor loses connectivity, do not reboot the core switch; call the plant supervisor first so the line can be paused safely. Then check the fibre link status on the core, and the UPS on the floor cabinet. Most outages are the floor cabinet UPS reaching end of life. Keep a spare UPS in the Halden store room.
