---
type: procedure
title: Reset a VPN certificate
tags: ["vpn", "access"]
---
When a colleague's VPN certificate expires or their laptop was rebuilt: open the VPN console, find the person by account name, revoke the current certificate, and issue a new one with a 12-month validity. Send the enrolment link through the helpdesk ticket, never by chat. The person installs it from the link within 24 hours; after that the link expires and the steps repeat.

Escalate to the network team if the console shows the account as disabled — that is an HR-driven state, not a VPN one.
