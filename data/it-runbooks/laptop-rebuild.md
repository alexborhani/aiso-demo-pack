---
type: procedure
title: Rebuild a laptop
tags: ["laptop"]
---
Back up the user's local folders to their cloud drive and confirm the sync finished. Boot from the imaging stick, choose the standard image for the model, and let it complete (about 40 minutes). Join the device to management, confirm disk encryption is on, install the role bundle, and hand it over with a fresh password-manager enrolment. Wipe the old disk only after the person confirms their files are present.
