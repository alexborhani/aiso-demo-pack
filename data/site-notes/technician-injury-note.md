A service technician, Tomasz Wierzbicki, cut his left hand on a casing edge while stripping a pump on bay 3. First aid on site, two stitches at the walk-in centre, back the next day on light duties. His doctor's note restricts him from lifting over five kilograms for three weeks. He has asked that the details stay with his line manager and People Operations. Bay 3 casing edges are to be deburred before the next job.
