title: Nordvik Water visit, 19 September
tags: ["customer", "sales"]
---
Nordvik's operations lead and their procurement manager visited the workshop. They were happy with the refurbishment turnaround and asked about a service contract covering all eleven of their booster stations. Indicative value discussed on the day: about 3.1 million a year over five years, with a discount of eight per cent if they sign before year end. Their procurement manager asked us not to circulate the figures until their board has seen the proposal. Follow-up owner: the Riverside service manager.
