---
type: procedure
title: Test rig start-up checklist
tags: ["workshop", "procedure"]
---
Before a test run: check the sump level and top up to the mark, confirm the discharge valve is a quarter open, walk the rig for loose guards, and log the pump serial in the rig book. Run at low speed for two minutes before ramping. Never leave a run unattended above 1,500 rpm. After the run, close the discharge valve fully, drain the sample line, and sign the rig book.
