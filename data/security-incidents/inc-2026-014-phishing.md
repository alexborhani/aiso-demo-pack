type: record
title: INC-2026-014: phishing campaign, April
tags: ["phishing", "incident"]
---
Forty-one staff received a message imitating the payroll provider. Three entered credentials on the fake page. Response: the three accounts were reset and their sessions revoked within 40 minutes, the sender domain was blocked, and the message was pulled from every mailbox. No data left the company. Lesson: the payroll provider's real messages should carry a known phrase; agreed and rolled out.
