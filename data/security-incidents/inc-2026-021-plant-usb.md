---
type: record
title: INC-2026-021: USB device on a plant controller
tags: ["plant", "incident"]
---
A contractor connected a personal USB stick to a Halden line controller to copy a firmware file. The device was clean, but the action broke the plant's removable media rule. The controller was checked and the contractor's site access was suspended pending retraining. Recommendation: USB ports on line controllers to be physically blocked by Q4.
