type: record
title: INC-2026-021: USB device on a plant controller
tags: ["plant", "incident"]
---
A contractor connected a personal USB stick to a Halden line controller to copy a firmware file. The device was clean, but the action broke the plant's removable media rule. The controller was checked. The contractor's badge (4471) was stopped at the cabinets pending retraining, but their system access (VPN account and controller login) is still active. Open action: revoke that system access; owner: the Security Lead. Open action: retraining sign-off; owner: the Halden shift lead. Recommendation: USB ports on line controllers to be physically blocked by Q4.
